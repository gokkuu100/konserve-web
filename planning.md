# Konserve Website Planning Document

## 🧭 Project Overview
Konserve is a waste management platform connecting Organizations, Collection Agencies, and Waste Buyers. The public website serves as an entry point for users, showcases Konserve’s environmental impact, and builds trust through transparency, storytelling, and live data.

---

## 🎯 Website Goals
- Attract and onboard new Organizations, Waste Buyers, and Collection Agencies
- Showcase real-time environmental impact through metrics
- Display social proof via testimonials and partner logos
- Provide an informative, modern, and mobile-responsive landing experience

---

## 🧩 Website Features (Complete List)

### 🔝 Hero Section (Landing Page)
- Bold headline with mission statement
- Background image, animation, or short video (eco/recycling theme)
- CTA buttons:
  - “Join as Organization”
  - “Join as Collection Agency”
  - “Join as Waste Buyer”

---

### 📈 Public Impact Metrics Section ("Our Impact So Far")
- ♻️ Total Waste Recycled (in kg)
- 👥 Number of Registered Users
- 🎁 Rewards Given Out (KES)
- 🏭 Number of Partnered Recycling Stations/Agencies
- 💬 Testimonials / User Reviews (carousel or grid)
- Live metric counters with animation on scroll
- (Optional) “See full impact report” PDF download

---

### 🧾 User Type Sign-Up Paths
- Section with three clearly defined user types:
  - Organization
  - Collection Agency
  - Waste Buyer
- Each with description, icon, and “Sign Up” button
- Leads to respective app portal or registration form

---

### 🛠️ How Konserve Works Section
- Simple visual flow (4 steps):
  1. Sign Up
  2. Schedule a Pickup
  3. Waste is Collected
  4. Waste is Tracked & Rewarded
- Use icons or illustrations for each step

---

### 🧬 About Konserve Section
- Brief intro to mission, story, and what Konserve stands for
- Coverage map of locations (counties served: Nairobi, Mombasa, Eldoret, etc.)
- Link to knowledge base or blog

---

### 🤝 Partner Logos / Affiliations
- Display logos of verified agencies, stations, or NGOs
- “Become a Partner” CTA button

---

### 💬 Testimonials / User Reviews
- Short quotes with star ratings and user type label
- Carousel, grid, or card layout
- Filter by user type (optional)

---

### 📩 Contact Section
- Contact form with fields:
  - Name
  - Email
  - Message
  - Type of Inquiry (dropdown: Partner Inquiry, Media, Support, General)
- Social media links (Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.)
- Location/address (optional)

---

### 🧠 Blog or Knowledge Center (Optional but recommended)
- Articles on:
  - Waste management tips
  - Recycling facts
  - Konserve’s journey & success stories
- Category tags and recent posts preview

---

### 📱 Design & UI/UX Requirements
- Fully responsive (mobile-first)
- Earth-tone color palette (greens, browns, neutrals)
- Clean sans-serif font (Inter, Poppins, etc.)
- Scroll animations (fade, counter animation)
- Sticky navigation bar
- Dark/light mode toggle (optional)

---

## 🔧 Backend/Technical Needs
- Metric counters to fetch live data from backend
- Testimonial management (manual or CMS-based)
- Form handling via backend or service (e.g., Formspree, Netlify, Firebase)
- Analytics integration (e.g., Google Analytics)

---

## 🔄 Planned Iterations (Phase 2+ Ideas)
- Gamification or reward tracker widget
- Partner dashboard or directory
- Waste category tracker breakdown
